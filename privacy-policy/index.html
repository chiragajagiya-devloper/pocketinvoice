Privacy Policy for Pocket Invoice

Last updated: 19-Aug-2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Pocket Invoice ("the App", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our application on Android and Windows.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: When you sign up or log in, we collect your name, email address, and password (stored securely and never in plain text) to create and manage your account. If you use "Forgot Password", we send a one-time password (OTP) code to your registered email to verify it's you.
  • Invoice and business data: Invoices, line items, headings, increase/deduction rows, party (customer) profiles, and bank profiles that you create inside the App, along with your chosen invoice/PDF layout settings (columns, totals, fonts, page padding, and similar formatting preferences). This data is stored locally on your device and is used to generate the PDF invoices you export or share.
  • Local backups: If you use "Backup All Invoices" / "Restore from Backup" with the destination set to "This Device", a backup file is saved to a folder on your own device (the default app documents folder, or a custom folder you choose). This file never leaves your device unless you move or share it yourself.
  • Google Drive backup data (optional): If you choose to link a Google account for backup, the App requests the drive.file permission, which only grants access to files and folders the App itself creates — never your existing Drive content. The App creates one visible folder named "Pocket Invoice Backups" in your Google Drive and stores a single backup file there, which you can see, open, or delete yourself at any time from Drive. The App cannot see, access, or modify any other files in your Google Drive.
We do not request access to your general Google Drive files, Gmail, Contacts, or any other Google data beyond the App's own backup folder described above.

2. How We Use Information

  • To create and authenticate your account, and to verify password-reset requests using an emailed OTP.
  • To let you create, customize, and generate invoices and related PDF documents within the App.
  • To save and restore your invoice data as a backup, either locally on your device or, only when you explicitly enable it, to your own Google Drive.
  • To send password-reset codes (OTP) and other account-related communication to your registered email address.

3. Data Storage and Security

Invoice data, party/bank profiles, and PDF layout settings are stored locally on your device. Account information is stored securely on our servers using industry-standard security practices. Local backups stay on your own device. Google Drive backup data is stored in your own Google account, inside a folder the App created, and is transmitted using HTTPS.

4. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google Sign-In / Google Drive API — used only for the optional Google Drive backup feature described above, on both Android and Windows. See Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. Your data is not shared with advertisers.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

You can delete your invoice data at any time from within the App. You can unlink your Google account backup at any time from the App's Profile settings, which stops any further access and leaves the existing backup folder in your Drive under your own control to keep or delete. To request full deletion of your account and associated server-side data, contact us using the details below.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
