Privacy Policy for Pocket Invoice Generator

Last updated: 17-Aug-2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Pocket Invoice Generator ("the App", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: When you sign up or log in, we collect your name, email address, and password (stored securely and never in plain text) to create and manage your account.
  • Invoice and business data: Invoices, line items, party (customer) profiles, and bank profiles that you create inside the App. This data is stored locally on your device and, if you choose to use PDF export, is used only to generate the PDF file you request.
  • Google Drive backup data (optional): If you choose to link a Google account for backup, the App requests the narrow drive.appdata permission only. This lets the App save and restore a single backup file inside a hidden, app-specific storage area of your Google Drive. The App cannot see, access, or modify any other files in your Google Drive.
We do not request access to your general Google Drive files, Gmail, Contacts, or any other Google data beyond the App's own private backup file.

2. How We Use Information

  • To create and authenticate your account.
  • To let you create, edit, and generate invoices and related documents within the App.
  • To back up and restore your invoice data across devices, only when you explicitly enable Google Drive backup.
  • To send password-reset codes (OTP) and account-related communication to your registered email address.

3. Data Storage and Security

Invoice data is primarily stored locally on your device. Account information is stored securely on our servers using industry-standard security practices. Google Drive backup data is stored in your own Google account and is encrypted in transit using HTTPS.

4. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google Sign-In / Google Drive API — used only for the optional backup feature described above. See Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. Your data is not shared with advertisers.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

You can delete your invoice data at any time from within the App. You can unlink your Google account backup at any time from the App's settings, which stops any further access to the backup file. To request full deletion of your account and associated server-side data, contact us using the details below.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
