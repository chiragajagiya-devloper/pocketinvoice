Pocket Invoice Generator

A simple mobile app to create, manage, and export professional invoices — right from your phone.

What this app does

Pocket Invoice Generator helps small business owners, freelancers, and shopkeepers create GST-ready invoices without needing a computer or accounting software. Add your customer (party) details, line items, taxes, and bank details, then generate a clean PDF invoice you can share instantly.

Key Features

  • Create and customize invoices with line items, taxes, and deductions
  • Save reusable customer (party) and bank profiles
  • Export invoices as PDF for sharing or printing
  • Optional Google Drive backup — keep your invoice data safe and move it to a new device without losing anything
  • Works fully offline for day-to-day invoice creation

Google Account Access

Pocket Invoice Generator only requests access to a private, app-specific storage area in your Google Drive (the drive.appdata scope) — solely to back up and restore your own invoice data. The app never accesses your personal files, Gmail, or Contacts.
